## A. Reading People (Profiling)

### 1. The Human Needs Map (Social Needs)
**What it is:** The main social needs that drive how a person acts with others. Chase lists them a bit differently in different clips:
- **Primary:** Significance (feel important), Acceptance (belong), Approval (be validated)
- **Secondary:** Intelligence (seen as smart), Pity (others understand what they went through), Strength / Power (seen as strong)

The key point: it is not that a person *is* these things. It is what they **need other people to see them as**.

**In the videos:**
- "If I know what you need to be seen as from other people, I know what was probably missing in your childhood." The "tough guy" truck with the MMA and Glock stickers "doesn't have a need to be powerful... He has a need for you to see him as powerful." [#14 What Someone Needs You to SEE](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/14%20-%20What%20Someone%20Needs%20You%20to%20SEE.md) [#16 Six Social Needs and the Childhood Triangle](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/16%20-%20Six%20Social%20Needs%20and%20the%20Childhood%20Triangle.md)
- The needs "reveal everybody's deeper fears and insecurities and their childhood issues. And you can spot these in 30 seconds or less." [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md) [#15 Looking Beyond the Mask](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/15%20-%20Looking%20Beyond%20the%20Mask.md) [#20 The Needs, Decision and Values Maps](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/20%20-%20The%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20and%20Values%20Maps.md)
- **Needs reveal fears:** "If I know that your need is significance, I automatically know... your biggest fears... feeling insignificant." [#11 Why We Hang Up on Sales Calls](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/11%20-%20Why%20We%20Hang%20Up%20on%20Sales%20Calls.md)
- Steven Bartlett's answers ("I want to pursue my potential") all point to **significance**, so praise like "you make a difference" hits harder than "everyone likes you." [#12 Breaking Down Steven Bartlett's Hidden Needs](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/12%20-%20Breaking%20Down%20Steven%20Bartlett%27s%20Hidden%20Needs.md)
- Chris Watts example: **significance and strength** stood out; strong needs point to deep insecurities (fear of being insignificant or weak). [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md)
- **Pity** need: "It's not that they want pity, it's that they want you to understand how bad they had it." Don't say "it's not that bad." Say "I don't know how you keep it together." [#22 Human Need 5 - Pity](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/22%20-%20Human%20Need%205%20-%20Pity.md)
- If you speak to the wrong need (acceptance to someone who needs significance), "they shut you out." [#15 Looking Beyond the Mask](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/15%20-%20Looking%20Beyond%20the%20Mask.md) [#20 The Needs, Decision and Values Maps](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/20%20-%20The%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20and%20Values%20Maps.md)

### 2. Decision Pillars (Decision Map)
**What it is:** The six ways people make choices: **Deviance, Novelty, Conformity, Necessity, Social, Investment**. They show what a person needs to hear to say yes.

**In the videos:** "These dictate every choice that a person makes... It shows you how they map reality and how they've been doing that since they're like ten." If you pitch **conformity** to someone whose pillar is **novelty**, they walk away and you blame "a numbers game." [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md) [#15 Looking Beyond the Mask](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/15%20-%20Looking%20Beyond%20the%20Mask.md) [#20 The Needs, Decision and Values Maps](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/20%20-%20The%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20and%20Values%20Maps.md)

### 3. Values (Values Map)
**What it is:** The future a person is chasing — their end goal: **Freedom, Recognition, Connection, Growth, Experiences, Information**.

**In the videos:** "This shows you what future a person is chasing. And sometimes this is sad... they place obstacles in front of themselves so they can continue the feeling of... chasing." If you offer **connection** to someone whose top value is **freedom**, "they recoil." [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md) [#15 Looking Beyond the Mask](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/15%20-%20Looking%20Beyond%20the%20Mask.md) [#20 The Needs, Decision and Values Maps](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/20%20-%20The%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20and%20Values%20Maps.md)

### 4. The Behavior Compass
**What it is:** A simple 3-step profile that combines a person's **Need + Decision Pillar + Value**. Chase says you can do it in under 60 seconds.

**In the videos:**
- "Once you are able to identify these three things, which you can do in under 60 seconds, you have more knowledge about how that person's mind works than most everyone that they know, even their own family." That's why they stress ethics. [#21 The Behavior Compass in Under 60 Seconds](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/21%20-%20The%20Behavior%20Compass%20in%20Under%2060%20Seconds.md)
- Used to profile Chris Watts (the "upper left quadrant" is the human needs). [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md)

### 5. Maslow's Pyramid, Used Backwards
**What it is:** Abraham Maslow's hierarchy of needs (survival, safety, love/belonging, esteem, self-actualization). Chase uses it to ask what a child had to *do* at each level between ages about 4 and 9.

**In the videos:** The top three levels (social, esteem, self-actualization) are "how needs are formed, how decisions are formed and how values are formed, in that order." When you watch adults, "we're seeing these little echoes in the present moment" from childhood. [#19 Maslow's Pyramid and the Behavior Compass](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/19%20-%20Maslow%27s%20Pyramid%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md) Also a "reverse" of Maslow's pyramid is used to explain the FATE model. ### 6. Six-Minute X-Ray (Rapid Behavior Profiling)
**What it is:** Chase's system (and book) for building a full behavior profile in about six minutes, from words, phrases and behavior.

**In the videos:** "Deeper than a two-month-long FBI behavior profile... in six minutes... in my head." With the profile, "I know what that person needs to hear... what's gonna make him feel threatened... it's no longer just a numbers game." [#17 The Six-Minute X-Ray](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/17%20-%20The%20Six-Minute%20X-Ray.md) He wrote three of Jack Kneel's insecurities on a notepad and they were right. ### 7. Transderivational Search
**What it is:** When someone's eyes move around in different directions while they think, they are searching through memories ("file cabinets") for an answer.

**In the videos:** When Steven Bartlett was asked "who are you," he looked up right, then left, then center: "that's called a transderivational search." [#12 Breaking Down Steven Bartlett's Hidden Needs](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/12%20-%20Breaking%20Down%20Steven%20Bartlett%27s%20Hidden%20Needs.md)

### 8. The Three Factors of Success: Self-Mastery, Observation, Communication
**What it is:** Chase says every success or failure comes down to three skills:
1. **Observation** — can you read the room and the person?
2. **Communication** — can you speak in a way that moves people?
3. **Self-mastery** — can you control yourself?

**In the videos:**
- "You can look at three factors every single time to determine why someone was successful or why they failed." [#1 Self-Mastery, Observation, Communication](transcripts/01%20-%20About%20Chase%20Hughes%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Ops%20Manual/01%20-%20Self-Mastery%2C%20Observation%2C%20Communication.md)
- For attorneys: success "is not gonna really come down to the law"; it's these three. If you lack self-mastery, "you're accidentally sending all the wrong gut feelings." Any failed sales call, hostage negotiation, or interrogation is missing one of the three. [#5 The Ellipsis Manual and the Three Skills](transcripts/01%20-%20About%20Chase%20Hughes%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Ops%20Manual/05%20-%20The%20Ellipsis%20Manual%20and%20the%20Three%20Skills.md)
- "My observation influences my communication." ## B. Body Language

### 9. The "Big Four" of Body Language
**What it is:** Instead of memorizing hundreds of signs, watch for: **Opening vs. Closing** and **Aggression vs. Uncertainty**.

**In the videos:** Watch whether the body starts or stops "protecting arteries." Example: "the moment that I mention interest rates, the person's arms gonna start moving in close to their body." [#23 Detecting Change Beats Memorizing Body Language](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/23%20-%20Detecting%20Change%20Beats%20Memorizing%20Body%20Language.md)

### 10. The 5 C's (Change, Clusters, Context, Culture, Checklist)
**What it is:** A ranked way to read behavior:
1. **Change** — a shift from their normal baseline (most important)
2. **Clusters** — several signs together, not just one
3. **Context** — crossed arms might just mean the room is cold
4. **Culture** — even one family can be its own culture
5. **Checklist** — memorizing single signs is the *least* useful

**In the videos:** "Being a detector of change is better than learning... If you're good at spotting change, you're better than the expert." "There is no body language for deception. None. What we really wanna spot are changes to that person's natural baseline." [#23 Detecting Change Beats Memorizing Body Language](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/23%20-%20Detecting%20Change%20Beats%20Memorizing%20Body%20Language.md)

### 11. Baseline and "Make It a Movie"
**What it is:** Know how a person normally acts, then look for change. Don't judge from one still picture.

**In the videos:** "Every time you see a still image, don't ever let that sit in your head. Make it a movie... cause you wanna watch for change." [#29 How to Spot a Narcissist on the First Date](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/29%20-%20How%20to%20Spot%20a%20Narcissist%20on%20the%20First%20Date.md) Also [#23 Detecting Change Beats Memorizing Body Language](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/23%20-%20Detecting%20Change%20Beats%20Memorizing%20Body%20Language.md) [#25 Blink Rate - An Easy Shift to Miss](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/25%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20-%20An%20Easy%20Shift%20to%20Miss.md) [#26 What You Were Taught About Reading People Is Wrong](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/26%20-%20What%20You%20Were%20Taught%20About%20Reading%20People%20Is%20Wrong.md)

### 12. Blink Rate
**What it is:** How often someone blinks. **Stress makes it go up. Focus and calm make it go down.**

**In the videos:**
- If the topic changes and they suddenly blink more, they are likely hiding stress about that topic. Then either skip the topic or ask more questions there. [#24 Blink Rate and Lip Compression](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/24%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20and%20Lip%20Compression.md) [#25 Blink Rate - An Easy Shift to Miss](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/25%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20-%20An%20Easy%20Shift%20to%20Miss.md)
- "If you're good at persuasion... you'll see the blink rate start to drop over time." [#24 Blink Rate and Lip Compression](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/24%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20and%20Lip%20Compression.md)
- Called a "micro behavior" quick win — "that is data." [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md) [#59 Observation, Communication, Self-Mastery and Your Frame](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/59%20-%20Observation%2C%20Communication%2C%20Self-Mastery%20and%20Your%20Frame.md)
- Chase says he knew "every word that causes your blink rate to go down" after two hours with Jack Kneel. [#18 Profiling Jack Kneel in Minutes](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/18%20-%20Profiling%20Jack%20Kneel%20in%20Minutes.md)

### 13. Lip Compression
**What it is:** Squeezing the lips together. It usually means **holding back an opinion, feeling, or information** — not necessarily lying.

**In the videos:**
- "How do you like your new job?" "Oh, it's great" + lips squeezed = "something's being held back." [#24 Blink Rate and Lip Compression](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/24%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20and%20Lip%20Compression.md)
- Venture capital tip: when a founder says "the financials are great" and compresses their lips — "just rewind. What were they just talking about right before?" [#27 Lip Compression in Venture Capital Pitches](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/27%20-%20Lip%20Compression%20in%20Venture%20Capital%20Pitches.md)
- "It doesn't mean you need to dig it out... but it's very powerful to notice." Works across all cultures. [#30 Lip Compression vs Lip Retraction](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/30%20-%20Lip%20Compression%20vs%20Lip%20Retraction.md)

### 14. Lip Retraction
**What it is:** When the lips (or a finger) go inside the mouth, past the teeth. It means the person **needs reassurance**.

**In the videos:** "Right away, I know that that person needs some kind of reassurance." [#30 Lip Compression vs Lip Retraction](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/30%20-%20Lip%20Compression%20vs%20Lip%20Retraction.md)

### 15. Tongue Jut
**What it is:** A small tongue push out between the lips, often seen right after a lie. Chase links it to baby behavior (pushing a nipple out), from Desmond Morris's theories.

**In the videos:** [#27 Lip Compression in Venture Capital Pitches](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/27%20-%20Lip%20Compression%20in%20Venture%20Capital%20Pitches.md)

### 16. Fake Openness (Spotting a Narcissist on a First Date)
**What it is:** Their **words** are open and vulnerable, but their **body** closes: hands pull in, arms draw back, they lean back, cover the stomach, or (mostly men) protect the groin.

**In the videos:** "Verbally they're physically closing off... you're seeing a fake openness and you're seeing a closed body." [#29 How to Spot a Narcissist on the First Date](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/29%20-%20How%20to%20Spot%20a%20Narcissist%20on%20the%20First%20Date.md)

### 17. The Chronological Story Red Flag
**What it is:** After a big event, an innocent person usually starts with the event itself. A story told in perfect time order, from long before the event, can be a red flag — unless the person was asked to tell it in order.

**In the videos:** Sarah Boone's story starts with art, wine, and hide and seek before the suitcase. "Most stories after any huge incident will start with the incident." [#86 Sarah Boone's Chronological Story](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/86%20-%20Sarah%20Boone%27s%20Chronological%20Story.md)

### 18. Learn One Behavior at a Time
**What it is:** Practice one cue until you notice it without trying, then add another — "like learning to drive a car."

**In the videos:** [#24 Blink Rate and Lip Compression](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/24%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20and%20Lip%20Compression.md) [#25 Blink Rate - An Easy Shift to Miss](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/25%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20-%20An%20Easy%20Shift%20to%20Miss.md)

## C. Authority, Confidence and Composure

### 19. The 5 Traits of Authority
**What it is:** The five things that make the human brain decide "I need to trust that person":
1. **Confidence** — feeling safe, not performing; "everything's gonna be okay"
2. **Discipline** — putting your future self first
3. **Leadership** — certainty in your thoughts and actions (not a title, and not even skill)
4. **Gratitude** — with perspective: can you zoom in (my family) and zoom out (my country, the solar system)?
5. **Enjoyment** — "the most magnetic human quality"; calmly enjoying the passing time

**In the videos:** [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md) [#32 The Authority Inventory - Find Your Lowest Point](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/32%20-%20The%20Authority%20Inventory%20-%20Find%20Your%20Lowest%20Point.md) [#35 He Just Said Mmm - Collapse vs Composure](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/35%20-%20He%20Just%20Said%20Mmm%20-%20Collapse%20vs%20Composure.md). Frank Abagnale (*Catch Me If You Can*) shows leadership is "certainty of self... Confidence and leadership do not have to correlate... with competence." [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md) Most stress comes from being "zoomed in" and not changing your camera angle. [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md)

### 20. The Hughes Authority Inventory (Authority Assessment)
**What it is:** A day-one checklist that scores you on the authority traits and finds your **lowest point**. Your lowest point is the thing holding you back the most, so it is the best place to start.

**In the videos:**
- "Wherever your lowest point is, that's what's keeping you from being successful in most conversations when you need to persuade somebody." [#33 Why You Get a Bad Gut Feeling About Someone](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/33%20-%20Why%20You%20Get%20a%20Bad%20Gut%20Feeling%20About%20Someone.md) [#35 He Just Said Mmm - Collapse vs Composure](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/35%20-%20He%20Just%20Said%20Mmm%20-%20Collapse%20vs%20Composure.md) [#36 The Real Problem Is Comfort](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/36%20-%20The%20Real%20Problem%20Is%20Comfort.md)
- A business owner whose staff mocked him worked on his lowest trait (confidence) and went from $600–800K to about $4 million a month. [#32 The Authority Inventory - Find Your Lowest Point](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/32%20-%20The%20Authority%20Inventory%20-%20Find%20Your%20Lowest%20Point.md)

### 21. The Five Areas That "Bleed Out" (Manufacturing Gut Feelings)
**What it is:** How you live off-camera leaks into your body language, breath and voice. The five areas: **Environment, Time, Appearance, Social life, Finances**. If these are a mess, people get a bad "gut feeling" about you even if you fake good posture.

**In the videos:** "I've got a eight foot pile of laundry... There's a part of our brain... reminding us, I'm faking it right now, and that comes across... We're manufacturing gut feelings in other people. So our job is to manufacture better gut feelings." [#33 Why You Get a Bad Gut Feeling About Someone](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/33%20-%20Why%20You%20Get%20a%20Bad%20Gut%20Feeling%20About%20Someone.md) [#35 He Just Said Mmm - Collapse vs Composure](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/35%20-%20He%20Just%20Said%20Mmm%20-%20Collapse%20vs%20Composure.md)

### 22. The Composure Scale: Collapse — Composure — Posturing
**What it is:** A scale with **collapse** on one side (making yourself small so others are comfortable) and **posturing** on the other (making yourself big to push people away). The goal is the middle: **composure**.

### 37. The PCP Model (Perception, Context, Permission)
**What it is:** The base of all influence, in order:

2. **Context** — that changes the situation, and so what behavior makes sense
3. **Permission** — which changes what they allow themselves to do

### 38. The FATE Model (Focus, Authority, Tribe, Emotion)
**What it is:** The "scripts our ancestors jammed into our DNA." What happens when each one is lost:
- Lose **Focus** → uncertainty
- Lose **Authority** → loss of safety, no direction
- Lose **Tribe** → loneliness (social media gives a fake tribe — "parasocial relationships")
- Lose **Emotion** → meaninglessness

When people lose resources (time, food, social), **impulse behavior spikes**. This is how fear-based selling and scarcity work. (The auto-captions often say "fake model.")

**In the videos:** [#57 The FATE Model and the Six-Axis Model](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/57%20-%20The%20FATE%20Model%20and%20the%20Six-Axis%20Model.md). ### 39. The Six-Axis Model
**What it is:** Six things you can raise in a person, and when to put each one first:
- **Focus** — when you need to be more memorable than persuasive
- **Openness** — when you need a big change from normal behavior
- **Connection** — when trust and emotion matter more than logic
- **Suggestibility** — when you need them to accept something unusual
- **Compliance** — when you want small yeses to build until they keep going on their own
- **Expectancy** — when excitement and trust are critical

**In the videos:** For a sales letter, his order is suggestibility, focus, connection, expectancy, compliance, and openness last ("I'm selling to your mask"). At a networking event, focus is already high and connection is the hardest. **What it is:** Bullying (in kids and adults) comes from:

### 61. "Pain as a Disguise for Virtue" (The Guilt Trick)
**What it is:** Your brain tricks you into thinking "I feel terrible, so I must be a good person." Chase says that's false: guilt keeps you at the center of "your own pity party" and robs others of a stable leader or present parent.

**In the videos:** Write down: **"I am using pain as a disguise for virtue."** [#51 Your Brain's Guilt Trick](transcripts/06%20-%20Shame%2C%20Guilt%20and%20Self-Forgiveness/51%20-%20Your%20Brain%27s%20Guilt%20Trick.md) [#49 Shame Is Not Morality (Full Talk)](transcripts/06%20-%20Shame%2C%20Guilt%20and%20Self-Forgiveness/49%20-%20Shame%20Is%20Not%20Morality%20%28Full%20Talk%29.md)

### 62. The Best Friend Test
**What it is:** Imagine your best friend confessing the exact thing you're ashamed of. Would you forgive them? "So why don't you forgive you?"

**In the videos:** [#51 Your Brain's Guilt Trick](transcripts/06%20-%20Shame%2C%20Guilt%20and%20Self-Forgiveness/51%20-%20Your%20Brain%27s%20Guilt%20Trick.md) [#49 Shame Is Not Morality (Full Talk)](transcripts/06%20-%20Shame%2C%20Guilt%20and%20Self-Forgiveness/49%20-%20Shame%20Is%20Not%20Morality%20%28Full%20Talk%29.md)
