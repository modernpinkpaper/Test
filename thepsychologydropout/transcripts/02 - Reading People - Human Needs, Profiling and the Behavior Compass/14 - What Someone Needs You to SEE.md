What if what someone needs you to SEE is more revealing than what they actually say they need? Chase Hughes explains that there are 6 social needs that can drive human behavior: • Significance • Approval • Acceptance • Intelligence • Pity • Power But there’s an important distinction: Someone may not actually need to be powerful — they may need you to see them as powerful. Think about the “tough guy” covered in signs, stickers, and symbols designed to communicate strength. The question isn’t necessarily “Is he powerful?” It’s: “Why does it matter so much that I know he is?” Once you understand the Human Needs Map, everyday behavior can start looking very different. #HumanNeeds #ChaseHughes #BehaviorOps #HumanBehavior #SocialDynamics
