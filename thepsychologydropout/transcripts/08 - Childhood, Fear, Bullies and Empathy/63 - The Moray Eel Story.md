I was stationed in Pearl Harbour, Hawaii, when I was 21 years old, and we would go snorkeling this place called Namame Bay. While we were down in the water, I got bit by a moray eel and it ripped into my muscles, my flesh. But I got out of the water, I got help, and I healed up. About a month later, I was complaining to my mentor about somebody that I worked with who was just a total asshole. And my mentor said, did he bite you in the arm? I'm like, no. What? Why would you say that? My mentor said, the eel bit you in the arm. You haven't told me that you're pissed about it, but this guy who's just slightly mean to you is getting you upset? I said, yeah, but the eel is just a creature doing what it does based off of who it became. And then he stared at me for like 15 seconds, until I realised that I was also talking about the person that I worked with that was making me upset. It is a creature who's just reacting based on how it grew up and how it evolved as a human being.
