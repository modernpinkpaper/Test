# Glossary

Simple definitions of the words and terms used in the videos, A to Z. Many are common words that **Chase Hughes defines in his own way**; those are marked ★. For full frameworks, see `Methods-Frameworks-Theories.md`.

---

**Acceptance** — A human need: wanting to belong and be included. One of the three primary needs. [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md) [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Alignment** ★ — When someone agrees with you because something inside them says "yes, that feels right." It dissolves resistance. The opposite of compliance. [#54 Alignment vs Compliance (Full Lesson)](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/54%20-%20Alignment%20vs%20Compliance%20%28Full%20Lesson%29.md) [#60 Alignment vs Compliance (Short)](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/60%20-%20Alignment%20vs%20Compliance%20%28Short%29.md)

**Approval** — A human need: wanting validation from others. [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md)

**Archetype** — A story pattern everyone knows (like David and Goliath). Once it's planted, the brain fills in the ending. [#84 David vs Goliath - Archetypes in Trial Psychology](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/84%20-%20David%20vs%20Goliath%20-%20Archetypes%20in%20Trial%20Psychology.md)

**Author** ★ — The person in a room who calmly shapes how everyone sees reality, instead of reacting. Chase says "authority" comes from this word. [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md) [#54 Alignment vs Compliance (Full Lesson)](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/54%20-%20Alignment%20vs%20Compliance%20%28Full%20Lesson%29.md)

**Authority** ★ — The quality that makes the brain decide "I need to trust that person." Made of five traits: confidence, discipline, leadership, gratitude, enjoyment. [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md)

**Authority Inventory** — A day-one checklist that scores you on the authority traits and finds your lowest one. [#32 The Authority Inventory - Find Your Lowest Point](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/32%20-%20The%20Authority%20Inventory%20-%20Find%20Your%20Lowest%20Point.md) [#33 Why You Get a Bad Gut Feeling About Someone](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/33%20-%20Why%20You%20Get%20a%20Bad%20Gut%20Feeling%20About%20Someone.md)

**AVERY** — Chase's 4-day program he describes as "a piece of software in the mind" to switch into a version of yourself without limits. [#9 AVERY - Software for the Mind](transcripts/01%20-%20About%20Chase%20Hughes%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Ops%20Manual/09%20-%20AVERY%20-%20Software%20for%20the%20Mind.md)

**Baseline** — How a person normally behaves. You read people by spotting changes from it. [#23 Detecting Change Beats Memorizing Body Language](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/23%20-%20Detecting%20Change%20Beats%20Memorizing%20Body%20Language.md)

**Behavior Compass** — A quick profile combining a person's need, decision pillar and value. [#21 The Behavior Compass in Under 60 Seconds](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/21%20-%20The%20Behavior%20Compass%20in%20Under%2060%20Seconds.md) [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md)

**Behavior Ops Manual (BOM)** — Chase Hughes's large reference book containing all his methods. [#2 Who Is Chase Hughes - The Behavior Ops Manual](transcripts/01%20-%20About%20Chase%20Hughes%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Ops%20Manual/02%20-%20Who%20Is%20Chase%20Hughes%20-%20The%20Behavior%20Ops%20Manual.md) [#1 Self-Mastery, Observation, Communication](transcripts/01%20-%20About%20Chase%20Hughes%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Ops%20Manual/01%20-%20Self-Mastery%2C%20Observation%2C%20Communication.md)

**Behavior Panel, The** — A group of body-language experts (Scott Rouse is one) who talk about behavior; mentioned when recommending books. [#79 The Psychopath Stare](transcripts/12%20-%20Psychopathy%20%28Scott%20Rouse%29/79%20-%20The%20Psychopath%20Stare.md)

**Blink rate** — How often someone blinks. Goes up with stress, down with calm focus. [#24 Blink Rate and Lip Compression](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/24%20-%20Blink%20Rate%20and%20Lip%20Compression.md)

**Bleed out** ★ — When how you live in private (messy room, money worries) leaks into your body language and voice. [#33 Why You Get a Bad Gut Feeling About Someone](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/33%20-%20Why%20You%20Get%20a%20Bad%20Gut%20Feeling%20About%20Someone.md)

**Childhood Development Triangle** — Three "primal rules" learned as a child: reward, safety, and friend (belonging). Adults still react from them. [#65 The Childhood Development Triangle](transcripts/08%20-%20Childhood%2C%20Fear%2C%20Bullies%20and%20Empathy/65%20-%20The%20Childhood%20Development%20Triangle.md)

**Clusters** — A group of body-language signs together. More reliable than one sign alone. [#23 Detecting Change Beats Memorizing Body Language](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/23%20-%20Detecting%20Change%20Beats%20Memorizing%20Body%20Language.md)

**Collapse** ★ — Making yourself small so others are comfortable (giving in, apologizing, discounting). One end of the composure scale. [#31 Compare Yourself on Comfort, Not Looks or Status](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/31%20-%20Compare%20Yourself%20on%20Comfort%2C%20Not%20Looks%20or%20Status.md) [#35 He Just Said Mmm - Collapse vs Composure](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/35%20-%20He%20Just%20Said%20Mmm%20-%20Collapse%20vs%20Composure.md)

**Comfort** ★ — Being more at ease than the other person in a conversation. Chase says it's the only thing worth competing on. [#31 Compare Yourself on Comfort, Not Looks or Status](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/31%20-%20Compare%20Yourself%20on%20Comfort%2C%20Not%20Looks%20or%20Status.md) [#36 The Real Problem Is Comfort](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/36%20-%20The%20Real%20Problem%20Is%20Comfort.md)

**Compliance** ★ — Going along out of fear or pressure. It only pauses resistance — "obedience with a little oven timer taped onto it." [#54 Alignment vs Compliance (Full Lesson)](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/54%20-%20Alignment%20vs%20Compliance%20%28Full%20Lesson%29.md)

**Compliance ladder / Micro-compliance** — Small requests (grab a pen, lean in) that lead step by step to bigger ones. [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md)

**Composure** ★ — The calm middle between collapse and posturing. A mix of the authority traits. [#35 He Just Said Mmm - Collapse vs Composure](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/35%20-%20He%20Just%20Said%20Mmm%20-%20Collapse%20vs%20Composure.md)

**Confidence** ★ — "A generalized expectation of positive or manageable outcomes." Not feeling amazing all the time. [#38 Confidence Is a Skill, Not a Trait](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/38%20-%20Confidence%20Is%20a%20Skill%2C%20Not%20a%20Trait.md) [#39 Confidence Is a World View](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/39%20-%20Confidence%20Is%20a%20World%20View.md)

**Conformity** — A decision pillar: deciding based on what others do. [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Context** — The situation. The same behavior can be fine in one context and unthinkable in another. Part of the PCP model. [#53 The PCP Model of Influence](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/53%20-%20The%20PCP%20Model%20of%20Influence.md) [#56 Can Hypnosis Make You Do Anything](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/56%20-%20Can%20Hypnosis%20Make%20You%20Do%20Anything.md)

**Culture** ★ — Habits shared by a group. Chase says even one family can be its own culture. [#23 Detecting Change Beats Memorizing Body Language](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/23%20-%20Detecting%20Change%20Beats%20Memorizing%20Body%20Language.md)

**Decision pillars** — Six ways people make choices: deviance, novelty, conformity, necessity, social, investment. [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Deviance** — A decision pillar: choosing what goes against the norm. [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Discipline** ★ — "Your ability to prioritise the needs of your future self." [#44 Discipline Means Putting Your Future Self First](transcripts/05%20-%20Discipline%2C%20Dopamine%2C%20Habits%20and%20Identity/44%20-%20Discipline%20Means%20Putting%20Your%20Future%20Self%20First.md) [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md)

**Dopamine** ★ — The brain chemical of pleasure. "Dopamine is not happiness. Dopamine is pleasure." [#43 Map Your Dopamine and Shift the Points](transcripts/05%20-%20Discipline%2C%20Dopamine%2C%20Habits%20and%20Identity/43%20-%20Map%20Your%20Dopamine%20and%20Shift%20the%20Points.md)

**Dopamine Map** — A 100-point map of where your pleasure comes from now, and where you want it to come from. [#46 The Dopamine Map](transcripts/05%20-%20Discipline%2C%20Dopamine%2C%20Habits%20and%20Identity/46%20-%20The%20Dopamine%20Map.md) [#43 Map Your Dopamine and Shift the Points](transcripts/05%20-%20Discipline%2C%20Dopamine%2C%20Habits%20and%20Identity/43%20-%20Map%20Your%20Dopamine%20and%20Shift%20the%20Points.md)

**Elicitation** — Getting information without asking direct questions, often by saying something people want to correct. [#52 Elicitation - Getting Info Without Asking Questions](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/52%20-%20Elicitation%20-%20Getting%20Info%20Without%20Asking%20Questions.md)

**Emotional subcontracting** ★ — Handing your self-worth over to other people's reactions. [#38 Confidence Is a Skill, Not a Trait](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/38%20-%20Confidence%20Is%20a%20Skill%2C%20Not%20a%20Trait.md)

**Enjoyment** ★ — Calmly enjoying the passing time. "The most magnetic human quality." [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md)

**Expectancy** — One of the six axes: excitement and expecting something good. [#57 The FATE Model and the Six-Axis Model](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/57%20-%20The%20FATE%20Model%20and%20the%20Six-Axis%20Model.md)

**FATE Model** — Focus, Authority, Tribe, Emotion — ancestral "scripts" that drive behavior. (Captions often say "fake model.") [#57 The FATE Model and the Six-Axis Model](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/57%20-%20The%20FATE%20Model%20and%20the%20Six-Axis%20Model.md) [#54 Alignment vs Compliance (Full Lesson)](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/54%20-%20Alignment%20vs%20Compliance%20%28Full%20Lesson%29.md)

**Focus** — One of the six axes (and part of FATE): attention. Prioritize it when you need to be memorable. [#57 The FATE Model and the Six-Axis Model](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/57%20-%20The%20FATE%20Model%20and%20the%20Six-Axis%20Model.md)

**Frame** ★ — Whose version of reality sets the meaning of a situation. Frame = Belief × Composure ÷ Neediness. [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md)

**Gnosis** — Direct, inner knowing (from the old Gnostic groups). Chase says you don't escape "the cage" through it — you escape by checking. [#71 The Truman Show and Waking Up](transcripts/09%20-%20Sartre%2C%20Performance%20and%20the%20Mask/71%20-%20The%20Truman%20Show%20and%20Waking%20Up.md)

**Gratitude** ★ — "Generalized positivity," with perspective: being able to zoom in and zoom out. [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md)

**Human Needs Map** — The social needs that drive behavior: significance, acceptance, approval (primary); intelligence, pity, strength/power (secondary). [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md) [#14 What Someone Needs You to SEE](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/14%20-%20What%20Someone%20Needs%20You%20to%20SEE.md) [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md)

**Identity** ★ — "What your nervous system predicts you will do under pressure." It produces your habits. [#47 Habits Come From Identity](transcripts/05%20-%20Discipline%2C%20Dopamine%2C%20Habits%20and%20Identity/47%20-%20Habits%20Come%20From%20Identity.md)

**Intelligence** — A human need: wanting to be seen as smart or competent. [#85 Profiling Chris Watts With the Behavior Compass](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/85%20-%20Profiling%20Chris%20Watts%20With%20the%20Behavior%20Compass.md)

**Investment** — A decision pillar: deciding based on return and value. [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Leadership** ★ — "Certainty in our thought and action." Not a title, and not necessarily skill. [#34 The 5 Traits of Authority](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/34%20-%20The%205%20Traits%20of%20Authority.md)

**Limbic system** — The emotional, "mammalian" part of the brain. Chase says real decisions happen here. [#55 Influence Is a Weapon](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/55%20-%20Influence%20Is%20a%20Weapon.md)

**Lip compression** — Pressing the lips together. Usually means holding back an opinion or feeling. [#30 Lip Compression vs Lip Retraction](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/30%20-%20Lip%20Compression%20vs%20Lip%20Retraction.md)

**Lip retraction** — Lips (or a finger) going inside the mouth. Usually means the person needs reassurance. [#30 Lip Compression vs Lip Retraction](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/30%20-%20Lip%20Compression%20vs%20Lip%20Retraction.md)

**Locus of control** — Whether someone feels in control of their life (internal) or that life happens to them (external). [#84 David vs Goliath - Archetypes in Trial Psychology](transcripts/13%20-%20True%20Crime%20and%20Trial%20Psychology/84%20-%20David%20vs%20Goliath%20-%20Archetypes%20in%20Trial%20Psychology.md)

**Mask** ★ — The version of yourself you show the world. Usually the opposite of what it hides. [#66 The Loudest Person Is Not the Strongest](transcripts/08%20-%20Childhood%2C%20Fear%2C%20Bullies%20and%20Empathy/66%20-%20The%20Loudest%20Person%20Is%20Not%20the%20Strongest.md) [#15 Looking Beyond the Mask](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/15%20-%20Looking%20Beyond%20the%20Mask.md)

**Mask slips, the** — When a psychopath's friendly act drops and they turn mean. (Scott Rouse) [#79 The Psychopath Stare](transcripts/12%20-%20Psychopathy%20%28Scott%20Rouse%29/79%20-%20The%20Psychopath%20Stare.md)

**Matching and mirroring** — Copying someone's way of talking and moving. Psychopaths do this so you feel you met a "kindred spirit." [#79 The Psychopath Stare](transcripts/12%20-%20Psychopathy%20%28Scott%20Rouse%29/79%20-%20The%20Psychopath%20Stare.md)

**Micro behavior** — A tiny, quick sign like a blink-rate change or lip squeeze. [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md)

**NCI / NCI University** — Chase's training courses (Level 1 and Level 2). [#14 What Someone Needs You to SEE](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/14%20-%20What%20Someone%20Needs%20You%20to%20SEE.md) [#28 Trust What You Notice (NCI Level 1)](transcripts/03%20-%20Body%20Language%20and%20Spotting%20Stress/28%20-%20Trust%20What%20You%20Notice%20%28NCI%20Level%201%29.md)

**Necessity** — A decision pillar: deciding only because you have to. [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Neediness** — How much you need from a situation. It weakens your frame. [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md)

**Novelty** — A decision pillar: choosing what's new. [#13 The Hidden Layer - Needs, Decision Pillars, Values](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/13%20-%20The%20Hidden%20Layer%20-%20Needs%2C%20Decision%20Pillars%2C%20Values.md)

**Openness** — One of the six axes: willingness to try something unusual. [#57 The FATE Model and the Six-Axis Model](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/57%20-%20The%20FATE%20Model%20and%20the%20Six-Axis%20Model.md)

**Operative** ★ — Chase's word for his students, borrowed from intelligence work. [#53 The PCP Model of Influence](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/53%20-%20The%20PCP%20Model%20of%20Influence.md) [#58 Four NCI Methods - Frame, Status, Micro-Behavior, Compliance Ladder](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/58%20-%20Four%20NCI%20Methods%20-%20Frame%2C%20Status%2C%20Micro-Behavior%2C%20Compliance%20Ladder.md)

**Parasocial relationship** — A one-sided "relationship" with someone online who doesn't know you. Gives a fake feeling of tribe. [#57 The FATE Model and the Six-Axis Model](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/57%20-%20The%20FATE%20Model%20and%20the%20Six-Axis%20Model.md)

**PCP Model** — Perception, Context, Permission — how all influence works, in that order. [#53 The PCP Model of Influence](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/53%20-%20The%20PCP%20Model%20of%20Influence.md) [#56 Can Hypnosis Make You Do Anything](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/56%20-%20Can%20Hypnosis%20Make%20You%20Do%20Anything.md)

**Perception** — How someone sees or understands what's going on. The first step in PCP. [#53 The PCP Model of Influence](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/53%20-%20The%20PCP%20Model%20of%20Influence.md)

**Permission** ★ — What someone allows themselves to do. Chase says influence is mostly "transferring permission." [#53 The PCP Model of Influence](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/53%20-%20The%20PCP%20Model%20of%20Influence.md)

**Permission slips** ★ — Milestones people wait for before feeling confident. Chase calls them placebos. [#37 Confidence - Stop Waiting for Permission](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/37%20-%20Confidence%20-%20Stop%20Waiting%20for%20Permission.md)

**Pity** — A human need: wanting others to understand how hard things were. [#22 Human Need 5 - Pity](transcripts/02%20-%20Reading%20People%20-%20Human%20Needs%2C%20Profiling%20and%20the%20Behavior%20Compass/22%20-%20Human%20Need%205%20-%20Pity.md)

**Placebo influence** ★ — Persuasion aimed only at logic. Chase says it doesn't change behavior. [#55 Influence Is a Weapon](transcripts/07%20-%20Influence%2C%20Persuasion%20and%20Hypnosis/55%20-%20Influence%20Is%20a%20Weapon.md)

**Posturing** ★ — Making yourself big and loud to push others away. Usually a fear behavior. [#31 Compare Yourself on Comfort, Not Looks or Status](transcripts/04%20-%20Confidence%2C%20Authority%20and%20Composure/31%20-%20Compare%20Yourself%20on%20Comfort%2C%20Not%20Looks%20or%20Status.md) [#66 The Loudest Person Is Not the Strongest](transcripts/08%20-%20Childhood%2C%20Fear%2C%20Bullies%20and%20Empathy/66%20-%20The%20Loudest%20Person%20Is%20Not%20the%20Strongest.md)
