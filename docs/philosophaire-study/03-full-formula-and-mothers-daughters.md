# The full script formula + script: Mothers and daughters

## Part 1: The full formula

### Step 1: Pick the feeling, not the topic

A topic is "mothers and daughters". A feeling is "why do we fight the most
with the person we love the most?" Always start from the feeling.

**Feeling test.** It must pass all 3:
1. Most people have felt it.
2. Few people have said it out loud.
3. You can say it in one sentence.

### Step 2: Pick an angle (one per video)

| Angle | What it does | Top-20 example |
|---|---|---|
| **Reframe** | Bad thing is secretly good | Rejection is protection (#1) |
| **Hidden reason** | "The reason you do X is Y" | You understand others because you want to be understood (#7) |
| **Expose** | Show what "they" do | How manipulators flip blame (#11) |
| **Rare people** | Praise a type of person | Kind people, "sun" people (#13, #19) |
| **Permission / comfort** | "You're allowed to..." / "You'll be okay" | Healing isn't a straight line (#8) |
| **Paradox** | Two things true at once | Both care, still can't connect (#3) |
| **Group identity** | "Always trust the..." | The oldest child (#14) |

### Step 3: Write the hook (first 3 seconds)

Rules:
- **One full sentence**, 8–20 words. It must make sense alone, like a quote.
- Start with a strong frame: *"The reason..."*, *"The saddest part is..."*,
  *"Some people..."*, *"There's a theory..."*, *"Always trust..."*, or a question.
- Use a **big word** in it: always, never, only, saddest, hardest, rare, real.
- It should make the viewer think **"that's me"** or **"that's them"**.
- No "hey guys", no "in this video", no intro.

Write 5 hooks. Keep the one that stings most.

### Step 4: Build the body (6 beats, ~150 words, ~60 seconds)

| Beat | Time | Words | Job | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0–5s | 10–20 | Stop the scroll | "The reason ___ is ___." |
| 2. Open it up | 5–15s | 20–30 | Say *why* it's true and why it hurts | "This is hard because..." / "Part of you still..." |
| 3. Make it real | 15–30s | 30–40 | 3 small, real moments | "The ___. The ___. The ___." |
| 4. The turn | 30–40s | 15–25 | Flip the meaning | "But it was never about ___. It was ___." |
| 5. Truth lines | 40–55s | 30–40 | 3–5 short lines to quote | "Not ___. ___." / "Some ___ are ___." |
| 6. Close | 55–65s | 8–15 | One line to save or send | A gentle order or a sting |

### Step 5: Use the emotional path

**Seen → Hurt a little → Understood → Hopeful (or wiser)**

- Beats 1–2: the viewer feels **seen**.
- Beat 3: the small real moments **hurt a little** (this is what makes them stay).
- Beat 4: the turn makes them feel **understood**.
- Beats 5–6: they leave **hopeful** or with a hard truth — and want to share it.

### Step 6: Sentence tools (use 5–7 per script)

1. **Not X. Y.** — "It's not stubborn. It's scared."
2. **Groups of three** — "The late calls. The long silences. The 'I'm fine'."
3. **Same start, repeated** — "Maybe she... Maybe she... Maybe she..."
4. **The flip (swap words)** — "You don't miss the place. You miss who you were in it."
5. **Say their secret thought** — "Part of you still thinks ___."
6. **Tiny real detail** — "a voice note you never played", "the kitchen at 11 p.m."
7. **Soft labels** — "Some people are lessons. Some are homes."
8. **"That's not X. That's Y."** — "That's not control. That's fear."
9. **The answer inside the doubt** — "If you have to ask, you already know."
10. **Fragments** — "No fight. No goodbye. Just distance."
11. **A calm order at the end** — "Call her." "Let it go." "Stay close."

### Step 7: Word rules

- **"You"** in almost every line. **"They"** for the other person. Almost never "I".
- Words a 10-year-old knows. No big or clever words.
- Sentences of about 8–10 words. 1 in 4 lines should be 5 words or less.
- Big words (always, never) in the hook. Soft words (maybe, sometimes) in the middle.
- No jokes, no slang, no emojis, no hashtags in the voice.

### Step 8: Rhythm (read it out loud)

- Pattern: **one longer line → two short lines.** Repeat.
- Put a pause (…) after every line that stings.
- The last line should be the shortest or the slowest.
- If you run out of breath on a line, cut it in two.

### Step 9: Score it before you record (need 8/10)

- [ ] Hook works alone as a quote
- [ ] Viewer can say "that's me" in the first 5 seconds
- [ ] 3 real, small moments (not general words)
- [ ] A clear turn ("But...")
- [ ] At least 2 "Not X. Y." lines
- [ ] One soft label or flip line
- [ ] "You" in most lines
- [ ] 140–170 words
- [ ] Last line is something people would screenshot or send
- [ ] Nothing in it is copied from another creator

### Step 10: Record and edit

- **Voice:** slow, low and calm, about 2.5 words per second. Smile a little on the last line.
- **Music:** soft piano or ambient, quiet under the voice.
- **Screen:** black 9:16, horizontal clip in the middle, film grain and soft glow.
- **Clips:** change every 3–6 seconds and match each line. Use your own, stock or AI-made clips.
- **Captions:** 1–3 words at a time, white, small, centered on the clip.

## Part 2: Script — Mothers and daughters

**Angle:** Hidden reason + Paradox
**Length:** about 150 words / 60 seconds

> **[HOOK]**
> The reason mothers and daughters fight the hardest… is because they're looking at themselves.
>
> **[OPEN IT UP]**
> She sees in you the girl she used to be. The one who wanted more. The one she put away so she could raise you.
> And you see in her the woman you're scared of becoming.
>
> **[MAKE IT REAL]**
> So small things turn into big things. The way you dress. The way she worries. The way you both say "I'm fine"… and close the door a little too hard.
>
> **[THE TURN]**
> But it was never about the dishes. Or the tone. Or the call you missed.
> It's two people who love each other in the exact same stubborn way.
>
> **[TRUTH LINES]**
> She wasn't trying to control you. She was trying to protect you from what hurt her.
> That's not pride. That's fear, dressed up as advice.
> One day you'll hear her voice come out of your mouth.
> And you won't be angry. You'll finally understand.
>
> **[CLOSE]**
> Call her… while you still can.

### Other hooks for the same video (for testing)

1. "Some daughters don't understand their mother until they become one."
2. "Your mother wasn't born a mother. She was a girl who had to grow up fast for you."
3. "The saddest part about mothers and daughters is that they're usually fighting the same fear."
4. "Always be gentle with your mother. She's learning how to love you in real time, too."

### Clip ideas (one per line)

| Line | Clip idea |
|---|---|
| Hook | Two women back to back, or a reflection in a window |
| "The girl she used to be" | Old-looking film of a young girl running in a field |
| "Scared of becoming" | A young woman looking into a mirror |
| "Small things" list | A door closing, a kitchen at night, a phone ringing |
| The turn | Rain on a window, quiet room |
| "Same stubborn way" | Two hands almost touching |
| "Her voice out of your mouth" | A woman holding a baby, soft light |
| Close | A phone screen that says "Mom", then fade to black |

### Note

Not everyone has a safe mother. If you want to be kind to those viewers, the
close can be softer: *"And if you can't call her, forgive the part of you
that still wishes you could."* Test both.
