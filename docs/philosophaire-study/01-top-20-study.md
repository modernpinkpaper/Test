## What the topics have in common

- **Relationships and love** (8 of 20): being chosen, letting go, love that can't connect.
- **Healing and moving on** (3): it's slow, quiet, not a straight line.
- **Friendship and rare people** (4): the forever friend, kind people, "sun" people.
- **You / identity** (3): you understand others but feel unseen; who you really are; the oldest child.
- **Morality and bad people** (2): hypocrisy, manipulators.

Every topic is something **almost everyone has felt**, but few people have
put into words. The video gives them the words.

## Visual style (what the video looks like)

- **Layout:** full 9:16 **black** screen. The clip sits in the middle as a
  smaller **horizontal box** (about 4:3). Black bars above and below.
- **Clips:** short animated scenes from films, cartoons and anime (soft,
  sad, dreamy moods: rain, sunsets, a person alone, city lights, silhouettes).
  Some old black and white film. A new clip every **3 to 6 seconds**, picked
  to match the line being said.
- **Color look:** film grain, soft glow, slightly faded color, dark edges
  (vignette). Looks like an old screen or VHS.
- **Captions:** only **1 to 3 words on screen at a time**, white, small clean
  sans-serif (sometimes italic), light shadow, placed in the **center of the
  clip**. Words appear in time with the voice.
- **No hook title card.** The first frame is just the clip + the first word.
- **Post captions/descriptions are empty.** No hashtags in the top videos.

## Audio style

- **Voice:** one calm, low, slow voice. About **150 words per minute**
  (2.5 words a second). No shouting, no hype. Pauses after key lines.
- **Music:** soft, sad piano or ambient bed under the voice. Most are
  "original sound". Others use slow instrumentals (for example *FOUND* by
  Zach Webb, *The Winner Is*, *Present*, *Cry*).
- **Length:** always **55 to 69 seconds** (just over 1 minute). About **125
  to 175 words** of script.

## Why these went viral (in short)

1. **The hook is a complete thought** that stings or surprises in 1 line.
2. **"You" language** — it feels like it's about the viewer.
3. **It names a feeling** people have but never said.
4. **Validation + a small hurt** — it comforts you, then hits a sore spot.
5. **Quotable lines** — people save and share to send to someone.
6. **Calm pace + pretty sad visuals** keep people watching to the end, and
   the 1-minute length gets rewatches.
