# Script: What a family member said that you'll never forget

**Angle:** Hidden reason + Permission
**Length:** about 150 words / 60 seconds

> **[HOOK]**
> The words that hurt you the most… usually came from someone who's already forgotten they said them.
>
> **[OPEN IT UP]**
> One sentence. At the dinner table. In the car. In the middle of a fight.
> They moved on by morning. But you didn't. You carried it into every room you walked into after.
>
> **[MAKE IT REAL]**
> "You're too sensitive." "Why can't you be more like your sister?" "No one's going to put up with that."
> Maybe it wasn't even loud. Maybe it was just a laugh… at the wrong moment.
>
> **[THE TURN]**
> It hurt so much because it came from family.
> Strangers can't reach that deep. Family doesn't have to aim. They already know where it will land.
>
> **[TRUTH LINES]**
> And the worst part is… you started saying it to yourself. In their voice.
> But something said on someone's worst day is not the truth about you.
> It wasn't a description. It was a wound, passed down.
>
> **[CLOSE]**
> You were allowed to be hurt by it. And you're allowed to stop believing it now.

## Other hooks to test

1. "Some of the deepest wounds you carry were given to you at the kitchen table."
2. "The reason you still remember what they said is because you trusted them not to say it."
3. "Family doesn't have to shout to break you. One sentence is enough."
4. "You're not too sensitive. You just heard it from the one person who should have protected you."

## Clip ideas

| Line | Clip idea |
|---|---|
| Hook | A person alone at a dinner table, empty chairs |
| "In the car" | Rain on a car window at night |
| Quotes list | A child sitting on stairs, a door half open |
| "A laugh at the wrong moment" | Blurry family photo, one face turned away |
| The turn | An arrow or a crack slowly spreading in glass |
| "In their voice" | A person looking into a mirror |
| "A wound, passed down" | An old photo fading into a newer one |
| Close | Sunrise through a window, fade to black |

## Tip

This topic makes people want to share their own story. Pin a comment like
"What's the sentence you still remember?" The answers are great ideas for
future videos.
