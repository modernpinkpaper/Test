This website does not use accounts, forms or tracking cookies of its own.

- **Hosting:** the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data (like IP address) for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Amazon links:** when you click an Amazon link, Amazon may set cookies so the purchase can be credited to us. See Amazon's privacy notice.
- **Purchases:** our products are sold through Payhip, which handles your payment and email securely. We receive your name and email so we can deliver your download. We never sell your data.

Questions? Use the contact option on our Payhip store page.
