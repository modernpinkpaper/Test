---
**Your download is instant.** Right after you pay, the download button shows on screen, and a copy of the link is emailed to you from Payhip.

### Can't find the email?
1. Check your **spam**, **promotions** or **updates** folder. Search your inbox for **"Payhip"**.
2. Make sure you look in the inbox of the email address you typed at checkout.
3. Still missing? Go to [payhip.com](https://payhip.com), and use **"Resend my purchase"** / **"Lost your download link?"** with the email you bought with. It sends the link again in seconds.

### Opening the file on a phone
- **iPhone:** tap the download, then tap the share icon → **Save to Files** or **Books**.
- **Android:** tap the download; it opens in your PDF viewer or Google Drive. It's also in your **Downloads** folder.

### Printing
Open the PDF on a computer and print at **"Actual size"** or **"Fit to page"**. You can print only the pages you want, as many times as you like, for your own home.

### Using it in GoodNotes or Notability
Import the PDF like any other document and write right on the pages.
